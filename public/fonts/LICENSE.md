# Fonts

The site sets Geist Sans and Geist Mono, from Vercel, under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1. They ship with the `geist` npm package rather than as
files in this folder, so there is nothing to vendor here.

https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

x.ai sets its display in Universal Sans, a paid licence from Family Type
that Ellelion LLC does not hold. It is not downloaded, hotlinked, or
imitated anywhere in this repository. Geist is the closest face we may
legally ship, and the resemblance stops at the skeleton.

General Sans and Fraunces were used earlier and have been removed.
