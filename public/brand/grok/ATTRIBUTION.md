# Grok logomark

`Grok_Logomark_Dark.svg` and `Grok_Logomark_Light.svg` are xAI artwork, copied
here unaltered from the official brand assets and used under
<https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines> (Feb 14, 2025).

Dark is the near-black mark for light backgrounds. Light is the white mark for
dark backgrounds. Neither file is recoloured, outlined, padded into a new
logo, or locked up with our own mark. The only approved phrase we set beside
it is "Created with Grok", with clear space between the words and the mark.
