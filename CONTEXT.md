# botteams.ai

botteams.ai publishes reusable Grok Bot recipes for one Bot or a team of Bots.

## Language

**Bot**:
A persistent, named Grok Bot that does one defined job.
_Avoid_: Agent, assistant, seat

**Bot roster**:
The ordered list of Bots in a recipe. A Bot recipe has one; a team recipe has two to six.
_Avoid_: Agent list, agents

**Team**:
A Bot roster of two to six Bots with at least one shared group chat.
_Avoid_: Pack

**Recipe**:
The public file that defines a Bot or team, its connectors, rooms, and routines.
_Avoid_: Plugin, installation
